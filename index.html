From concept to shipping product

A custom design moves through five steps, from the first simulation to production release.

  1. Simulate

    Linear and nonlinear circuit simulation, 3D EM analysis, and system-level cascade analysis.

  2. Lay out

    MMIC, package, and RF board layout using foundry process design kits.

  3. Assemble

    Prototype builds with epoxy die attach and wirebonding, in house.

  4. Measure

    S-parameters, noise figure, power, and phase noise to 50 GHz, on wafer or in a fixture.

  5. Release

    Mask set, wafer probe, and production release, from one prototype to more than 1,000 units.
