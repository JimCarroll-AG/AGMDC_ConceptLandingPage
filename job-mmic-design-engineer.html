MMIC Design Engineer

A senior role that takes MMIC designs from specification through tape-out, test, and production, working directly with customers and foundries on a small, hands-on team.

About AGMDC

AGMDC is the RF and microwave design division of AmpliTech Group, Inc. (NASDAQ: AMPG). We design MMICs and RF subsystems for SATCOM, defense, 5G, space, and quantum computing from our lab in Allen, Texas.

What you will do

  • Lead MMIC development from customer requirements through production
  • Design GaAs and GaN MMICs from microwave through mmWave frequencies
  • Run linear and nonlinear circuit simulation and 2.5D and 3D EM verification
  • Prepare tape-outs and work with foundries on process and wafer issues
  • Integrate MMICs into packaged modules and subsystems such as LNBs
  • Lead lab characterization and correlate measurements with simulation
  • Support proposals and technical discussions with customers
  • Mentor junior engineers, and publish or present technical work

What you need

  • BS in EE with 8+ years of experience, MS with 6+ years, or PhD in RF/microwave with 4+ years
  • A record of MMIC tape-outs that went into production
  • MMIC amplifier design experience, plus some experience with switches, phase shifters, attenuators, or mixers
  • Working knowledge of GaAs and GaN technologies
  • Proficiency with Microwave Office or ADS, and with an EM tool such as AXIEM, Analyst, Sonnet, Momentum, or HFSS
  • Hands-on microwave measurement experience
  • Strong written and verbal communication, including with customers

Nice to have

  • LNA and mmWave design for SATCOM, 5G, or defense
  • mmWave power amplifier design
  • SiGe or CMOS RFIC design
  • Module and package design with EM co-simulation
  • Published papers or conference presentations, for example at IEEE IMS or BCICTS

Work authorization

Work on ITAR-controlled programs requires U.S. person status (citizen, permanent resident, asylee, or refugee).
