How do GaAs, GaN and InP compare?
GaAs is commonly used for low-noise and general microwave circuits; GaN offers high power density and voltage capability; InP can be attractive for very high-frequency and low-noise receivers. The best choice depends on the actual process, frequency, thermal constraints and economics.

What is a satellite low-noise block downconverter (LNB)?
An LNB amplifies a weak received microwave signal and converts it to a lower intermediate frequency (IF) for transport and receiver processing. Important specifications include input noise temperature, conversion gain, local oscillator stability, image rejection and spurious response.

Link to this answer

What is the difference between an LNA and an LNB?
A low-noise amplifier (LNA) amplifies an RF signal while minimizing added noise. An LNB combines low-noise amplification with frequency conversion and typically includes filtering and a local oscillator. An LNB therefore delivers an IF output rather than simply amplified RF.

How do I specify an LNB frequency plan?
Provide the RF input band, desired IF output band, LO frequency or allowable LO options, spectral inversion preference, image rejection, reference frequency and any unwanted signal constraints. For fundamental mixing, IF = |RF − LO|. Both injection choices should be evaluated against image and spur requirements.

How does LNB noise temperature relate to noise figure?
Equivalent input noise temperature T (kelvin) and noise figure NF (dB) are related by NF = 10 log₁₀(1 + T/290), using a 290 K reference. A lower input noise temperature generally improves receiver sensitivity; overall system performance also depends on antenna noise, feed losses and preceding components. Use the calculator below.

When should I use bare die rather than packaged RF components?
Bare die can minimize interconnect length and permit compact chip-and-wire integration, but requires controlled die handling, attachment and bonding. Packaged parts simplify board assembly and handling, while package parasitics and thermal behavior must be considered.
