Senior RF Test Systems Engineer

Build and run the automated test stations that characterize our LNAs, MMICs, and RF subsystems, using Python to turn bench measurements into fast, repeatable production test.

About AGMDC

AGMDC is the RF and microwave design division of AmpliTech Group, Inc. (NASDAQ: AMPG). We design MMICs and RF subsystems for SATCOM, defense, 5G, space, and quantum computing from our lab in Allen, Texas.

What you will do

  • Design, build, and maintain Python-based automated test stations
  • Automate VNA S-parameter measurements, including calibration, de-embedding, and fixtures
  • Develop noise figure and gain measurements using Y-factor methods
  • Automate power sweeps for P1dB, Psat, OIP3, and efficiency
  • Test 5G NR signals for ACLR, EVM, and linearity
  • Build tools for data collection, analysis, and reporting
  • Maintain test limits, procedures, and data records
  • Troubleshoot test hardware and support root-cause analysis
  • Keep equipment calibrated and help specify new test capability

What you need

  • BS in Electrical Engineering or a related field
  • 5+ years in RF test, measurement, or hardware engineering
  • Hands-on experience with VNAs, noise figure analyzers, spectrum analyzers, signal generators, and power meters
  • Strong Python skills for instrument control and data analysis
  • Solid understanding of S-parameters, noise figure, compression, and linearity
  • Working knowledge of 5G NR and modulated-signal measurements
  • Strong analytical, troubleshooting, and communication skills

Nice to have

  • Experience testing LNAs, MMICs, or RF modules at microwave and mmWave frequencies
  • On-wafer or fixtured test and calibration experience
  • Familiarity with Keysight, Rohde & Schwarz, or similar instruments
  • Experience with Git and with test data dashboards or databases
  • US Person status for work on ITAR-controlled programs
