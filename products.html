Off-the-shelf MMICs and low noise blocks

AGMDC offers 98 standard parts from DC to 50 GHz. Most are available as bare die for chip-and-wire assemblies, many also come in DFN or QFN packages, and our low noise blocks ship as complete modules. They are backed by the same RF engineers, test lab, and foundry experience as our custom work, so any part can be screened, modified, or packaged for your program.

Don't see the exact part you need?

Most AGMDC engagements start that way. We design custom MMICs, packaged parts, RF boards, and integrated subsystems to a customer's requirements, and we can screen, modify, or package any part in this guide.

Our low noise blocks are a good example. The three standard units cover C, X, and K band, and semi-custom variants can be built quickly to a different frequency plan, gain, or waveguide interface.

Tell us your frequency band, gain and noise target, power level, package preference, and timeline. We will come back with a practical path to working hardware.
