2nd Shift Engineering Technician

Run our automated RF test stations on 2nd shift, keep the lab productive after hours, and move prototype and production hardware through test.

About AGMDC

AGMDC is the RF and microwave design division of AmpliTech Group, Inc. (NASDAQ: AMPG). We design MMICs and RF subsystems for SATCOM, defense, 5G, space, and quantum computing from our lab in Allen, Texas.

What you will do

  • Run RF tests on LNAs, MMICs, and RF subsystems, and record and report the results
  • Set up and calibrate VNA, noise figure, and power test stations
  • Assemble, solder, and rework RF boards and modules
  • Troubleshoot failing units and test setups, and document what you find
  • Work with engineers to improve test procedures, fixtures, and throughput
  • Follow ESD and handling procedures and keep the lab organized
  • Write shift handoff notes covering test progress, open issues, and equipment status

What you need

  • Associate's degree in electronics or a related field, or equivalent experience
  • 2+ years of RF test experience in a production or engineering setting
  • Hands-on experience with VNAs, signal generators, spectrum analyzers, and power sensors
  • Understanding of RF and DC circuits, and the ability to read schematics and datasheets
  • Ability to work independently on 2nd shift
  • Clear written and verbal communication, and working knowledge of Microsoft Office

Nice to have

  • Experience testing RF semiconductors or microwave modules
  • Experience with automated test equipment, including Python-based test software
  • Soldering and rework experience on RF PCBs (IPC certification is a plus)
  • US Person status for work on ITAR-controlled programs
Location
Allen, TX
Type
Full time or part time, 2nd shift
